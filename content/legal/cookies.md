# Cookie Policy

**HwinNwin** uses cookies and similar technologies to provide a better user experience.

## What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website.

## How We Use Cookies
- To enable website functionality
- To analyze traffic (via Plausible Analytics)
- To remember your preferences

## Managing Cookies
You can disable or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some site features may not work properly without cookies.
