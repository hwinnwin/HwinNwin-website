Built with intention across seven phases of enhancement:

### Phase 1: Privacy as Sacred Boundary
Analytics that respects consciousness. Consent is not negotiable. Do Not Track is honored. Every data point requires permission.

### Phase 2: Connection as Service
Contact forms that carry intention. Email systems that honor communication. Spam protection that maintains boundaries.

### Phase 3: Content as Transmission
Blog posts that bridge consciousness. Markdown that becomes meaning. Words chosen for frequency, not just information.

### Phase 4: Visibility as Responsibility
SEO that serves discovery. Meta tags that truthfully represent. Sitemaps that guide seekers to wisdom.
