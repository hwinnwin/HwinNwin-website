# Privacy Policy

**HwinNwin Pty Ltd** (ABN pending) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the **Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)** under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit hwinnwin.com or use our services.

---

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

When you engage with us, book a consultation, or use our services, we may collect:

- Name and email address
- Phone number
- Business name and contact details
- Project requirements and goals you share with us
- Payment and billing information
- Communication preferences

### Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we automatically collect:

- IP address and browser type
- Device information and operating system
- Pages visited and time spent on pages
- Referring website addresses
- Usage patterns via privacy-focused analytics (Plausible Analytics)

We do **not** use invasive tracking tools. Our analytics are cookie-free and privacy-respecting by default.

### Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from third-party platforms you connect to our services, such as social media accounts or business tools, only with your explicit consent.

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Respond to enquiries and provide our consulting, automation, and creative services
- Process transactions and send related information
- Deliver project work including digital strategy, automation systems, and creative assets
- Send administrative updates, project communications, and invoices
- Improve our website, services, and client experience
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
- Protect against fraud or unauthorised access

We will **never** sell your personal information to third parties.

---

## AI and Automated Processing

Our services may involve AI-assisted tools for content creation, automation workflows, and analytics. When using these features:

- AI-generated outputs are created based on your inputs and project requirements
- Your data may be processed by third-party AI providers to deliver our services
- We do not use your personal content to train AI models
- You retain ownership of all deliverables created for you
- AI processing is subject to the same data protection standards as all other data handling

---

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information with:

- **Service Providers:** Third-party vendors who assist in operating our business (hosting, payment processing, project management tools)
- **Professional Partners:** When collaborating on your project, only with your consent
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by Australian law, regulation, or legal process
- **Business Transfers:** In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

All third-party service providers are bound by contractual obligations to keep your information confidential.

---

## Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access, including:

- Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Secure hosting with reputable providers
- Access controls and authentication measures
- Regular review of security practices

Data may be stored in Australia or with secure international providers who maintain equivalent or higher privacy standards. Where data is transferred overseas, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

## Cookies and Tracking

We use **Plausible Analytics**, a privacy-focused analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. For more details, see our [Cookie Policy](/hwin/legal/cookies).

Essential cookies may be used for website functionality such as session management and preferences.

## Your Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete information
- **Request deletion** of your personal data
- **Withdraw consent** where processing is based on consent
- **Lodge a complaint** with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) if you believe your privacy has been breached

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the details below.

## Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. When a project concludes and retention periods expire, we will securely delete or anonymise your data.
