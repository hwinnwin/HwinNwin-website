# Cookie Policy

**HwinNwin** uses cookies and similar technologies to provide you with the best possible experience on our website. This policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can control your preferences.

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide essential functionality, improve your experience, and understand how you use our site.

## Cookie Categories

We categorize our cookies into four types, giving you full control over what you accept:

### Essential Cookies (Always Active)
These cookies are necessary for our website to function properly and cannot be disabled.

**What they do:**
- Enable basic website functionality like navigation and form submissions
- Maintain your session when you're logged into owner areas
- Remember your cookie preferences
- Provide security features to protect against attacks

**Examples of data stored:**
- Session identifiers
- Security tokens
- Cookie consent choices
- Form data during submission

### Analytics & Performance Cookies
These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website so we can improve your experience.

**What they do:**
- Track anonymous usage statistics via Plausible Analytics
- Measure website performance and loading times
- Identify which pages are most popular
- Help us fix errors and optimize content

**Examples of data collected:**
- Page views and time spent on pages
- Browser type and screen resolution
- Geographic location (country/region level only)
- Referring websites and search terms

**Third-party services:**
- **Plausible Analytics**: A privacy-focused analytics service that doesn't track individual users or use invasive tracking techniques. Learn more at [plausible.io](https://plausible.io)

### Functional Cookies
These cookies enhance your experience by remembering your preferences and settings.

**What they do:**
- Remember your language and region preferences
- Save your customized settings and layout choices
- Maintain preferences across multiple visits
- Provide personalized content recommendations

**Examples of data stored:**
- Language preferences
- Theme settings (light/dark mode)
- User interface customizations
- Remembered form inputs

### Marketing & Advertising Cookies
We currently do not use marketing cookies, but this section describes what they would do if implemented in the future.

**What they would do:**
- Show you relevant advertisements
- Track campaign effectiveness
- Prevent duplicate ad displays
- Measure conversion rates

**Note:** We do not currently use any marketing or advertising cookies on our website.

## Your Choices and Rights

### Cookie Consent Banner
When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner with these options:
- **Accept All**: Enables all cookie categories for the best experience
- **Essential Only**: Only enables necessary cookies required for functionality
- **Customize**: Choose exactly which categories you want to enable

### Managing Your Preferences
You can change your cookie preferences at any time by:
- Clicking the "Cookie Preferences" link at the bottom of any page
- Using the cookie settings button in your browser

### Browser Controls
You can also manage cookies directly in your browser settings:
- **Chrome**: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
- **Firefox**: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari**: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data
- **Edge**: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Cookies and site data

**Important:** Disabling essential cookies may prevent some features from working properly.

## Cookie Retention

- **Session cookies**: Deleted when you close your browser
- **Persistent cookies**: Stored for specific periods based on their purpose:
  - Cookie preferences: 1 year
  - Analytics data: 26 months (Plausible Analytics retention)
  - Functional preferences: 6 months

## Privacy and Data Protection

We are committed to protecting your privacy:
- We do not sell your personal information to third parties
- Analytics cookies are processed anonymously and cannot identify you personally
- We use privacy-focused services that comply with GDPR and other data protection laws
- You have the right to withdraw consent for optional cookies at any time
