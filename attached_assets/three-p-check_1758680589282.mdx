# The 3P Check in Action

Every system we design runs through the **3P Check**:

- **Power**: Will this actually move the needle?
- **Balance**: Does it fit sustainably with the team and culture?
- **Prosperity**: Will the effects compound into growth and clarity?

This check keeps us anchored in calm, purposeful systems.
