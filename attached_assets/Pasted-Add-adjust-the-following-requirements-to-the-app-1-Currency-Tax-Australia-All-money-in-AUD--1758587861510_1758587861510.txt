Add/adjust the following requirements to the app:

1) Currency & Tax (Australia)
- All money in AUD. Display "AUD $" and include GST (10%).
- Show Subtotal (ex-GST), GST (10%), and Total (inc GST) in summaries and on the quote.

2) Owner-in-the-loop Approval
- Customer never sees the dollar amount until owner approves.
- Flow: Customer submits ➜ internal provisional estimate ➜ owner reviews/edits ➜ owner toggles "Approved" ➜ customer gets final quote link/PDF.
- Simple owner access: 4–6 digit OWNER_PIN required to open the dashboard.

3) Persistence (DB)
- Use SQLite for MVP.
- Data models:
  - Quote(id ULID or UUID, createdAt, updatedAt, status: 'new'|'approved'|'sent',
          customer{name, phone, email}, vehicle{rego, make, model, year, paint},
          items[ {panel, severity, partsCost, blend:boolean} ],
          rates{labourRate, materialsPerPanel, partsMarkup, metallicMultiplier, pearlescentMultiplier, minJob},
          calc{repairHrs, paintHrs, labour, materials, parts, subtotalExGST, gst, totalIncGST, confidence: 'high'|'low'},
          ownerNotes, customerLinkSlug)
  - Settings(singleton): default rates and theme (logoUrl, primaryColor).

4) Calculation Rules
- Continue using current heuristics; add:
  - GST = 10% of subtotalExGST.
  - Confidence flag = 'low' if: >2 panels OR any 'severe' OR missing critical photos.
  - Blend toggle per item; blending adds 0.6 paint hr and 50% materials for each blended panel.

5) Image Handling
- Validate uploads: warn if < 640px shorter side OR file > 8MB OR likely blurry (use browser image decode + edge variance heuristic).
- Store file names/URLs in DB; for MVP keep in /uploads (server) and serve statically.
- Abstract storage so we can swap to Cloudinary/S3 later.

6) Sharing & Export
- After approval:
  - Generate a branded HTML quote and a downloadable PDF (simple server-side HTML-to-PDF lib).
  - Send email via EmailJS (client) OR a minimal server mailer (e.g., nodemailer) using ENV vars.
  - Create a public customer link: /q/:slug (read-only view).

7) Legal/UX
- Include disclaimer on forms and quotes: "Provisional estimate only. Final price may change after physical inspection."
- Require checkbox "I confirm photos are representative".
- Display a small "Confidence" badge (High/Low) to the owner.

8) Rates & Theme Persistence
- Owner can edit rates in dashboard; persist in Settings table.
- Theme variables (logoUrl, primaryColor) editable; apply to header, buttons, and PDF.

9) Minimal Analytics (owner-only)
- Show counts: total quotes, approved quotes, average total, last 7 days submissions.

10) Testimonial Capture (owner-only)
- After sending, show a one-click "Request Testimonial" email with a link to a simple form (name, rating 1–5, comment). Store results in DB.

11) API & Routes
- REST endpoints:
  - POST /api/quote (create from customer form)
  - GET  /api/quote/:id (internal)
  - PATCH /api/quote/:id (owner edits)
  - POST /api/quote/:id/approve (requires OWNER_PIN)
  - GET  /q/:slug (public read-only customer view)
  - GET  /api/quote/:id/pdf (returns PDF)
  - GET/POST /api/settings (owner)
- Add simple OWNER_PIN middleware for owner routes.

12) Env & Config
- .env: OWNER_PIN=123456, EMAIL_HOST/USER/PASS (if using nodemailer), SITE_URL (e.g., https://lee888.com.au).
- Fail gracefully if email creds are missing (still allow PDF download and copy link).
